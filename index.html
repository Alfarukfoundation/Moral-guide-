About Us

Vision: To build a morally responsible and value-driven society.

Mission: To educate, inspire, and empower individuals—especially youths—with moral values and life skills.

Objectives: Promote moral awareness, provide mentorship, discourage social vices, encourage leadership and responsible citizenship.
